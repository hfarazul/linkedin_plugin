# Pitch

Agentic Labs builds AI agents. For an operations leader that means agents that
take repetitive, manual work out of the operation and integrate with the
software the team already runs. OrionQ is the published example.

# Who we are writing to

Senior operations leaders at established companies: people who own how the
work gets done, and who are likely being asked what AI should change about it.

# Tone

Professional and direct, peer to peer. Curious rather than knowing: the emails
ask about their operation, they do not diagnose it. Never preachy.
