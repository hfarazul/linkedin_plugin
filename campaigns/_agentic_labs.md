makes them persuasive.

# Anti-claims — DO NOT say

- Any collective-seniority figure for the team or its leadership (see above).
- "Cheaper than agencies" — we are not a price play.
- A fixed engagement length or team-size equivalence.
- A quantified result for Bespoke, ChatForge, Zia, or any client logo — none is
  published.
- "We work with founders coming from finance/healthcare/retail" — generic and
  untrue without context.
- "We'll attend your event" / "I'll be at <conference>" — never invent
  attendance.
- "I'd love to chat" / "Let's connect to learn more" — banal CTAs.
- Never invent a client name, a headcount, a timeline, a success rate, or a
  description of how we spend our week.

# Tone

Engineer-to-founder. Specific over general. Short over long. Curious rather
than knowing. Reference one real thing the prospect has done, one real thing we
have built, one specific question. No agency-speak. No flattery as the opener.
Never preachy, never a know-it-all.
